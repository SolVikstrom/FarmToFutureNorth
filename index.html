02 · Store

In my role as a store, I want to buy and display food, so that I can sell it with a good profit per metre of shelf.

I run a grocery store, choose the food we sell, and decide how to display it.

Threshold

Standardisation

As a store, I want products that fit my shelf. I look for GS1 barcodes and pack sizes that fit, and a beautiful product that does not fit does not get a place from me.

How might we

meet the shelf rules without erasing the local story?

Threshold

Logistics integration

As a store, I want goods to arrive without extra work. I take them through the central warehouse, or as an approved direct delivery. Anything else is extra work for me.

How might we

enter the chain’s logistics and still keep the local story intact?

Threshold

Returns and terms

As a store, I want the terms agreed before I buy. I ask for unsold goods, campaign prices, and a purchase price that stays steady through the season.

How might we

write terms for unsold food that a small producer can actually live with?

Threshold

Turnover and margin

As a store, I want the shelf to pay for itself. The product has to leave before it becomes waste, and the margin has to cover the metre. A slow jar costs me more than an empty space, and if the numbers do not work per metre, I do not keep the product.

How might we

help local food leave the shelf before it spoils?

How might we

lower the cost per metre of shelf for the artisan food producer?

What if

several producers share the cost for one metre?

What if

the producer paid only for the weeks the food is on the shelf?

03 · Municipality

In my role as a municipality, I want to buy food, so that I can serve nutritious meals every day.

I cook for school kitchens, health care, and social care.

Threshold

Rules and procurement

As a municipality, I want to buy inside the rules. I buy only when specifications, framework agreements, and certification are met.

How might we

pass public procurement rules without shutting out small producers?

Threshold

Delivery reliability

As a municipality, I want every meal to leave on time. I keep the cold chain, and an empty order is not an option for me.

How might we

promise the clock, the cold chain, and a full delivery?

Threshold

Budget and price per portion

As a municipality, I want each portion to stay inside a fixed cost. A fair price for the producer still has to fit inside my number.

How might we

fit a fair producer price inside a fixed cost per portion?

Motivation

Administrative ease

As a municipality, I want buying to be simple to run. I prefer few invoices and one combined delivery. Many small suppliers arriving separately are harder for me.

How might we

make one hub as easy to use as one wholesaler?

Motivation

Volume and scale

As a municipality, I want one steady order through the term. My kitchens share it, even when one kitchen’s need shifts.

How might we

let several kitchens share one steady order?

04 · Tourist

In my role as a tourist, I want to experience authentic local flavours and food culture, so that I can connect with the place and enjoy something I cannot find at home.

I visit new places and explore local food, restaurants, and traditions.

Motivation

Authenticity

As a tourist, I want to meet the place through the food. I stop for local ingredients, traditions, and the people behind the food. The product alone is not the whole reason I am here.

How might we

highlight the personal stories of producers?

What if

we helped the producer show the real home and the personal service?

What if

we coached producers to become foodfluencers, with AI to help them make the content?

Motivation

Arctic identity

As a tourist, I want the food to feel of this place. I look for pristine nature, the seasons, wild ingredients, and a northern way of life.

How might we

make Arctic origin visible and easy to recognise?

Motivation

Souvenirs and memories

As a tourist, I want to take the place home. I look for a jar or a bite I can talk about later.

How might we

make local food an attractive souvenir?

Threshold

Easy to find

As a tourist, I want the food where I already am. I look in hotels, restaurants, attractions, airports, and online.

How might we

make Arctic food easy for tourists to discover?

Threshold

Value must be obvious

As a tourist, I want the extra price to feel obvious. I pay more for a memorable experience when the origin and the story are easy for me to understand.

How might we

make the extra price feel obvious, in a language a visitor can read?

05 · Primary producer

In my role as a primary producer, I want to expose my harvest to potential buyers, at the right time, in the right place, with the right story, so that it is easy for them to buy it.

I grow and harvest food and sell it to shops, restaurants, and local customers.

Threshold

Season and volume

As a primary producer, my harvest and volume are governed by the weather and the season, but customers are used to the same range all year, and contracts want steadiness.

How might we

reshape market and contract expectations around year-round consistency, turning natural seasonal variations into a unique value proposition rather than a commercial liability for the primary producer?

Threshold

Price and effort

As a primary producer, it is difficult to capture fair financial value for the labour and resources invested, while simultaneously navigating and managing an optimal mix of sales channels.

How might we

help primary producers capture premium returns with minimal operational drag?

What if

we made selling directly take less of the producer’s effort?

What if

Threshold

Few buyers

As a primary producer selling raw harvest or bulk carcasses, I face a consolidated wholesale market with few buyers, leaving me with virtually zero bargaining power to negotiate sustainable prices.

How might we

help primary producers break free from monopsonistic wholesale pricing and access buyers who value quality over bulk commodity rates?

Threshold

Transport and distribution

As a primary producer in a sparsely populated region, geographic isolation from conventional cold chains and centralized distribution networks forces me into costly, time-consuming individual delivery runs that erode margins and limit access to distant buyers.

How might we

connect remote primary producers to distant retail and institutional markets without letting logistics overhead eat up their margins?

In my role as an artisan food producer, I want to achieve wide market reach to ensure that my business thrives.

I process and sell directly to kitchens, restaurants, and consumers.

Threshold

Leftovers

As an artisan food producer of a made product, what is left over, or sold as animal feed, is not food a person will buy.

How might we

turn that into food a person will buy?

What if

we made it into a sausage, a drink, or a flour?

Threshold

Price

As an artisan food producer of a made product, when I rely on large buyers, the price is too low.

How might we

get a better price than the large buyers pay?

What if

we helped the producer process the food and sell it directly?

Threshold

Clean nature

As an artisan food producer of a made product, international customers look for the clean nature, and for food they can take home. The product has to carry that.

How might we

let the product carry the clean nature, so they can take it home?

What if

we made the clean nature obvious on something they can carry home?
