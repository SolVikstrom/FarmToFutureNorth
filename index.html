Farm to Future North

Four stories, told in the first person. Choose one and read from the role downward.

01 · Store customer

In my role as a store customer, I want to buy food and ingredients, so that I can meet my own and my loved ones’ need for food.

Motivation

Habit and routine

As a store customer, I return to products I already like. When I look for a new recipe, I turn to cooks I trust, food apps, and cooking shows.

How might we

introduce a new brand, or a new food, into a habit that already feels settled?

What if

we connected local influencers and food enthusiasts with local food producers, or helped producers use AI to generate engaging content?

What if

large stores like ICA could promote local food in their recipes and apps?

Motivation

Convenience

As a store customer, I want everything in one place, in the shop or online. Extra time and extra errands matter to me.

How might we

make local food the easy, one-stop choice rather than a separate errand?

Motivation

Preferences and values

As a store customer, diet, health, and the climate are important to me. I also want a feeling of belonging with the place the food comes from.

How might we

create incentives to support local food that feel as natural as the habit itself?

What if

we started a national Local Food Day, like Cinnamon Bun Day?

Threshold

Sensory quality

As a store customer, I need to see and feel that the food is fresh: the look, the smell, and whether it will last at home.

How might we

let people judge freshness before they have to trust a label?

Threshold

Budget

As a store customer, the food has to fit my household budget. I judge the value at the moment I buy.

How might we

adjust the price so it feels attractive, while the producer still earns a living?

What if

supporting local became as strong a statement as the Pink Ribbon, for example through monthly donors?

What if

municipalities supported local food producers with themed nudge coupons for residents?

02 · Store

In my role as a store, I want to buy and display food, so that I can sell it with a good profit per metre of shelf.

Threshold

Standardisation

As a store, I expect GS1 barcodes and pack sizes that fit my shelf. A beautiful product that does not fit does not get a place from me.

How might we

meet the shelf rules without erasing the local story?

Threshold

Logistics

As a store, I want goods to arrive through the central warehouse, or as an approved direct delivery. Anything else is extra work for me.

How might we

enter the chain’s logistics and still keep the local story intact?

Threshold

Returns and terms

As a store, I need unsold goods, campaign prices, and a purchase price that stays steady through the season agreed before I buy.

How might we

write terms for unsold food that a small producer can actually live with?

Motivation

Turnover

As a store, I need the product to leave my shelf before it becomes waste. A slow jar costs me more than an empty space.

How might we

help local food leave the shelf before it spoils?

Threshold

Margin

As a store, my margin has to pay for the shelf. If the numbers do not work per metre, I do not keep the product.

How might we

earn the shelf when the margin has to carry the space?

03 · Municipality

In my role as a municipality, I want to buy food, so that I can serve nutritious meals every day.

I cook for school kitchens, health care, and social care.

Threshold

Rules and procurement

As a municipality, I can buy only when specifications, framework agreements, and certification are met. The process is strict for me, and hard for a small producer to enter.

How might we

pass public procurement rules without shutting out small producers?

Threshold

Delivery reliability

As a municipality, my meals leave at fixed times. The cold chain cannot break, and an empty order is not an option for me.

How might we

promise the clock, the cold chain, and a full delivery?

Threshold

Price per portion

As a municipality, I have a fixed maximum cost per portion. A fair price for the producer still has to fit inside my number.

How might we

fit a fair producer price inside a fixed cost per portion?

Motivation

Administrative ease

As a municipality, I prefer few invoices and one combined delivery. Many small suppliers arriving separately are harder for me.

How might we

make one hub as easy to use as one wholesaler?

Motivation

Volume and scale

As a municipality, my kitchens together can carry a steady order through the term, even when one kitchen’s need shifts.

How might we

let several kitchens share one steady order?

04 · Tourist

In my role as a tourist, I want to experience authentic local flavours and food culture, so that I can connect with the place and enjoy something I cannot find at home.

Motivation

Authenticity

As a tourist, I stop for local ingredients, traditions, and the people behind the food. The product alone is not the whole reason I am here.

How might we

highlight the personal stories of producers?

Motivation

Arctic identity

As a tourist, pristine nature, the seasons, wild ingredients, and a northern way of life are what make this food feel of the place to me.

How might we

make Arctic origin visible and easy to recognise?

Motivation

Souvenirs and memories

As a tourist, I want a jar or a bite I can take home, and talk about later.

How might we

make local food an attractive souvenir?

Threshold

Easy to find

As a tourist, I need the food where I already am: hotels, restaurants, attractions, airports, and online.

How might we

make Arctic food easy for tourists to discover?

Threshold

Value must be obvious

As a tourist, I will pay more for a memorable experience, but only when the origin and the story are easy for me to understand.

How might we

make the extra price feel obvious, in a language a visitor can read?
